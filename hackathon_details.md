# Quantum Healthcare Hackathon

## Overview
The goal of this hackathon is to use quantum techniques to improve a classical machine learning approach or to develop a quantum model that outperforms a baseline on relevant metrics. 

The quantum integration must serve a clear purpose, addressing a limitation of the classical approach and supporting measurable performance gains. Classical baselines should be chosen comparably similar to the quantum approach, whether through similar data preprocessing, feature engineering, or model architectures. Participants must deliver a functional codebase and a comparative evaluation demonstrating both approaches.

## Challenge & Evaluation Steps

1. **Define the healthcare problem**
   Explain the problem, its relevance, the data used, and the intended outcome.
2. **Establish a strong classical baseline**
   Build or reproduce a suitable classical machine learning approach. Credit existing work and document its configuration and performance. This establishes a reference performance on the chosen healthcare problem.
3. **Develop the quantum contribution**
   Use quantum techniques to improve the classical model or develop a quantum model. Explain why the quantum component is appropriate, which limitation it addresses, and implement the quantum contribution.
4. **Evaluate both approaches fairly (Shared evaluation)**
   Compare both approaches under consistent evaluation conditions. Use appropriate metrics and consistent data. Present the results, improvements, and limitations clearly.

## How Submissions are Assessed
Submissions will be assessed on the quantum approach’s performance relative to its classical baseline, alongside the ingenuity, depth, and soundness of the chosen problem-solving approach.

## Prepare Your Submission
To successfully submit your project, you must provide the following:
* **Functional codebase:** Implement the classical and quantum approaches.
* **Comparative evaluation:** Show both models' results.
* **Explain your approach:** Explain the healthcare problem, methods, and the purpose of the quantum integration.
* **Reproduce the work:** Include relevant settings, dependency information, and instructions needed to reproduce the work.
